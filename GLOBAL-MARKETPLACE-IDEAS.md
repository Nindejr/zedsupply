# Marketplace ideas beyond Amazon and Takealot

Reviewed 1 November 2026 using the marketplaces' own documentation or company
filings. Availability, fees and guarantees vary by country and product. The
adaptations below are recommendations for Zed Supply, not claims of partnership
or promises that those services are available in Zambia.

| Marketplace / region | Observed pattern and source | Useful adaptation for Zed Supply | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alibaba.com / China, global B2B | Detailed purchase terms, quality and dispatch commitments, and evidence-based dispute handling. [Buyer Central](https://activity.alibaba.com/page/how_it_wroks.html), [Trade Assurance](https://seller.alibaba.com/trade-assurance?locale=en) | Preserve approved specifications, selected options, prices and accepted terms on the order. Next, add structured RFQs for SME quantities, delivery location, warranty and inspection criteria. | Server-priced quote/confirmation implemented; fuller RFQ negotiation remains proposed. |
| AliExpress / China, global retail | Choice groups products under a managed service proposition, with conditions varying across markets. [Alibaba Group explanation](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-1696296090350911488) | Offer explicit service-eligibility labels backed by actual fulfilment data. Show which items support local pickup or a confirmed delivery service, rather than a site-wide promise. | Retain item/store eligibility; do not invent free delivery or guaranteed dates. |
| JD.com / China | A specific on-time service has stated eligibility and compensation conditions. [JD help centre](https://help.jd.com/user/issue/321-4495.html) | A future local delivery promise should have measurable deadlines, exclusions and a funded remedy. First collect delivery events and evidence. | Shipment timeline and controlled transitions implemented; compensation remains a business decision. |
| Pinduoduo / China | Its company filing describes optional team purchasing through shared purchase groups. [PDD annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1737806/000110465926050727/pdd-20251231x20f.htm) | Consider transparent group procurement for school, office or farm supplies, with a clear quantity threshold, deadline and automatic cancellation/refund if unmet. | Research-stage only. Existing bulk tiers are simpler; avoid compulsory sharing and manufactured urgency. |
| Flipkart / India | Eligible open-box deliveries let customers inspect certain products before acceptance. [Flipkart guide](https://stories.flipkart.com/open-box-delivery-flipkart-customer-trust) | Pilot an inspection checklist for high-value local orders: correct model, visible damage and included components, followed by authenticated receipt evidence. | Evidence records implemented; inspection service remains off until sellers and couriers agree to perform it. |
| IndiaMART / India B2B | Its official app listing describes buyer requirements and supplier conversations. [IndiaMART app listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.indiamart.m) | Make “request sourcing” a structured brief with product, quantity, unit, destination and timing. Compare supplier quotes on total delivered cost and agreed specifications. | Recommended next SME workflow; no supplier database or response promise fabricated. |
| TradeIndia / India B2B | Requirement posting and supplier discovery support procurement. [TradeIndia](https://www.tradeindia.com/) | Reusable procurement requests, quote expiry and purchase-order references would complement the current bulk-price and stock-planning features. | Proposed after authenticated seller workflows. |
| Shopee / Southeast Asia | Shopee Guarantee is tied to payments through specified platform channels and has release conditions. [Shopee terms](https://help.shopee.ph/portal/4/article/77272) | Show protection only for eligible transactions through a validated provider. A WhatsApp conversation alone must not imply protected payment. | Existing protection remains disabled outside previews; payment integration is gated. |
| Coupang / South Korea | Delivery and returns benefits are supported by its operating model and membership conditions. [Coupang overview](https://www.aboutcoupang.com/what-we-do/), [membership explanation](https://www.aboutcoupang.com/the-wow-factor-a-look-inside-the-incredible-benefits-of-wow-membership-2/) | Prioritize reliable coverage, predictable delivery and easy returns before loyalty or membership pricing. | Fulfilment/support foundations implemented; no “same-day everywhere” claim. |
| Mercado Libre / Latin America | Its filing describes protection connected to marketplace payments, non-delivery and item problems. [Company filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1099590/000109959026000017/meli-20260331.htm) | Integrate payment, shipment and support evidence around the same order identity, with settlement and refund reconciliation. | Durable payment attempts, private evidence, support escalation and refund ledger implemented; actual provider reconciliation still needs the selected provider. |

## Recommended sequence

First validate the connected account → quote → order → payment → delivery →
support path. Next, pilot structured procurement requests and inspection at local
handover. Introduce delivery promises only after operational data supports them.
Group purchasing, loyalty schemes and cross-border buying should wait until
fulfilment, refunds and supplier controls are dependable.

Useful differentiation is clear evidence, accurate total costs and good service
on a weak connection. Do not copy another marketplace's logos, artwork, wording,
guarantee names or interface wholesale. Keep Zed Supply's existing dimensional
artwork and Zambia-first workflows.
