Where should we deliver?

We add your delivery location to your WhatsApp order, so we can confirm delivery to your area and the delivery fee before you pay.

For example "Plot 12, near the filling station". Please don't share GPS coordinates.

If you leave this unticked, we keep your location only until you close this browser tab. It's never sent to us except in WhatsApp messages you choose to send.

            How Zed Supply works

            1. Find or request it

              Search, shop by category or send us a request for what you need.

            2. Checkout on WhatsApp

              Your cart is sent to us as an itemised order. We confirm the final price and delivery.

            3. We see it through

              We connect your order to the right seller or supplier and support it through to fulfilment.

            About Zed Supply

            Zed Supply is a Zambia-based e-commerce business building an online marketplace for everyday products, business supplies and other goods, for households, professionals, SMEs and organisations. We started with a simple idea: make buying and sourcing products easier.
