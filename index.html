The problem we solve

Buying shouldn't mean searching everywhere

Buying often means checking multiple sellers, comparing prices manually and arranging fulfilment separately. Buyers may have to search different shops, social-media pages and suppliers before making a purchase.

For organisations, sourcing routine supplies can involve the same repeated search. Zed Supply is built to bring discovery, choice and ordering into one marketplace.

The usual way

  • Contact several shops, pages and suppliers
  • Compare prices by hand
  • Arrange fulfilment separately

With Zed Supply

  • One place to search, compare and order
  • Household, consumer and business purchases together
  • One contact from request to fulfilment
