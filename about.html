About us

Making buying and sourcing easier

A Zambia-based e-commerce business, based in Lusaka.

Who we are

Zed Supply is a Zambia-based e-commerce business building an online marketplace for everyday products, business supplies and other goods.

We want to make it easier for people and organisations to find what they need, compare options and buy from one place.

We are based in Lusaka and intend to serve customers across Zambia, with scope to expand beyond Zambia as the business grows.

Our purpose

We started with a simple idea: make buying and sourcing products easier.

Customers should not have to contact several suppliers just to find an item, compare prices or arrange a purchase. Zed Supply aims to bring products, sellers and buyers together through one accessible online marketplace.

Who we serve

Zambian households, professionals, SMEs and organisations that want a simpler way to shop or source products online.

Why choose us

Zed Supply is being designed around the realities of buying and sourcing products in Zambia, rather than simply copying an overseas marketplace model.

  • A Zambia-first marketplace, with scope to serve regional buyers and sellers.
  • One marketplace intended to span household, consumer and business purchasing.
  • Access for both individual and organisational buyers.
  • Opportunities for Zambian suppliers and businesses to reach more customers.
