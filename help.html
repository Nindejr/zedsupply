Courier options

At checkout you can say where your order is going (within your town, to another town in Zambia, or outside Zambia) and pick a courier, or let us arrange it. You can choose from every courier licensed by ZICTA, Zambia's postal and courier regulator. The best-known are below; the ones most used by Zed Supply sellers come first.

  • Zampost EMS (public postal operator): across Zambia and to over 190 countries.
  • DHL Express (ZICTA licensed): to and from other countries.
  • FedEx (ZICTA licensed): same-day within a city, across Zambia and abroad.
  • Mercury Express Logistics (ZICTA licensed): international and domestic.

All licensed couriers

Every operator on ZICTA's register of licensed postal and courier operators. Domestic licences cover anywhere in Zambia; local licences cover one district only.

Turn on JavaScript to search the list, or see ZICTA's licensed operators register (opens in a new tab).

Which one suits you?

  • Same town, today: a motorbike courier or on-demand app is usually fastest for small parcels.
  • Another town: an established courier with offices at both ends, so the parcel can be tracked and collected.
  • Outside Zambia: an international express courier or Zampost EMS. Allow time for customs at both ends.
  • Large or heavy items: ask for a vehicle or cargo service, and confirm the price for the size and weight first.

Getting your parcel safely: tips for buyers

  • Give a full delivery location with a landmark, and a phone number you'll answer.
  • Ask for the tracking or waybill number, and keep it until your order arrives.
  • Open and check the parcel when it arrives. If it's damaged, take photos and tell the courier and us before you sign.
  • Pay only the delivery fee we confirm on WhatsApp. Don't pay anyone who contacts you unexpectedly asking for a "release" or "clearance" fee: check with us first.
  • Parcels from outside Zambia may attract customs duty and VAT from the Zambia Revenue Authority, and you may need to collect them for inspection.

Sending parcels: tips for sellers

  • Use a licensed courier. Couriers in Zambia are licensed by ZICTA; check the licensed postal and courier operators register (opens in a new tab). Bus companies need a courier licence to carry parcels too.
  • Agree the cost first. Confirm the delivery fee, and who pays it, before you dispatch.
  • Pack for the journey. A strong box, padding around the item, waterproof wrapping in the rainy season, sealed with tape, and labelled with the order number. Mark fragile items.
  • Keep evidence. Photograph the item and the sealed parcel, and keep the waybill. Send the tracking number to the buyer.
  • Get proof of delivery, such as a signature, photo or delivery code.
  • Insure valuable items and declare their true value.
  • Check restricted items. Batteries (including power banks), liquids, chemicals and perishables have special rules, especially by air.
  • Exports: include a customs declaration and a commercial invoice, and ask the courier or a clearing agent about Zambia Revenue Authority requirements.
  • Have a backup. Keep two couriers for each route you send to, and tick them in your Seller Centre.

How paying works

  1. Check out on WhatsApp, and choose how you'd like to pay (or decide later).
  2. We confirm the final price and delivery fee with you on WhatsApp.
  3. We send you payment instructions for the way you chose, including the exact amount and the name the payment goes to.
  4. You make the payment yourself: you approve mobile money on your own phone, a bank transfer in your own bank app or at the bank, and a card payment on our payment provider's secure page. Then send us the confirmation.

Always check that the name shown on your phone matches the name we gave you before you approve a payment.

Keeping your payment details safe

  • Zed Supply will never ask for your card number, card security code (CVV), PIN or Mobile Money PIN on this website, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp.
  • If anyone claiming to be Zed Supply asks for them, don't share them. Tell us straight away.
  • Our website blocks card numbers from being sent through its forms, so they can't be sent to us by mistake.
  • When online card payments open, you'll pay on our payment provider's secure page. Zed Supply won't see or store your full card details.

Returns and buyer protection

Interim policy, last updated 28 October 2026. We'll review it with legal advice; any change won't affect orders placed before it. Nothing here limits your rights under Zambian consumer protection law.

Every seller on Zed Supply agrees to this policy as a minimum. Sellers may offer more, and say so on their listings.

If something is wrong with your order

For example, it's faulty, damaged in delivery, the wrong item, size or colour, not as described, or missing parts.

  • Tell us within 7 days of delivery: report the problem or message us on WhatsApp with your order number and photos.
  • We or the seller arrange to collect it, or tell you how to send it back. You don't pay to return it.
  • You choose a replacement, a repair or a full refund, including any delivery fee you paid. If it can't be replaced or repaired, you get a refund.
  • If the parcel looks damaged when it arrives, photograph it before opening, and tell the rider if you can.

If you change your mind

  • Return it within 7 days of delivery, unused and in its original condition and packaging, with any tags and accessories.
  • Tell us first, so we can arrange the return. You pay the return delivery unless the seller agrees otherwise.
  • We refund the price of the item. The original delivery fee isn't refunded.

Unless they're faulty or not as described, these can't be returned because you changed your mind:

  • food, drinks and other perishable goods
  • health, beauty and personal-care items once opened or unsealed, and underwear or swimwear
  • items made, altered or sourced specially for you, including items "sourced on request"
  • software, SIM cards, airtime and vouchers once opened or used

Refunds

  • We check the returned item, then refund you within 14 days, the same way you paid: mobile money to the same number, a bank transfer to your account, or back to the same card.
  • If you paid a price you offered and the seller accepted, we refund that price.

Warranties

Some items come with a manufacturer's warranty, shown in their specifications. We'll help you claim on it. A warranty is extra to this policy, not instead of it.

Buyer protection

If a seller doesn't respond, or you and the seller can't agree, Zed Supply looks at the evidence from both sides and decides. If we decide a refund is due, we make sure you get it.

Cancelling an order

  • Before the seller packs it: open the order in Your orders and choose Cancel this order, or message us on WhatsApp. There's no charge. If you've already paid, we refund you the same way you paid.
  • Once it's packed or on its way: ask us. The seller may still be able to stop it. If not, you can refuse it at the door or return it once it arrives, under the policy above.

Complaints

If something has gone wrong with an order, a seller, a listing or a delivery, we want to hear about it, whether or not you've bought anything.

How to complain

  • Make a complaint. You can also start one from an order, a product page ("Report a problem with this listing") or a store page.
  • Or message us on WhatsApp, or use the contact details below.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint and give you a reference number, starting ZC-.
  2. If it's about a seller, we pass it to them with your order details, unless you ask us not to, and they reply to us.
  3. We reply to you within 2 working days with what we've found and what we propose: a replacement, repair, refund or return.
  4. If you're not happy with the outcome, ask us to review it. A manager looks at it again.

If you're still not satisfied

  • For problems with goods or services as a consumer: the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC).
  • For problems with a courier or postal service: the Zambia Information and Communications Technology Authority (ZICTA), which licenses couriers.
  • For a problem with a payment itself: your mobile money provider or bank.

Sellers and complaints

Sellers must answer complaints within 2 working days. When complaints against a seller are upheld again and again, we warn them, remove listings, or pause or close their store. We take a listing down straight away while we look into a report of a counterfeit or unsafe product.

What can't be sold on Zed Supply

  • Medicines, whether prescription or over the counter, including veterinary medicines. First-aid supplies, health monitors and medical equipment can be listed.

About Zed Supply

Zed Supply is a Zambia-based e-commerce business building an online marketplace for everyday products, business supplies and other goods. We want to make it easier for people and organisations to find what they need, compare options and buy from one place.

We are based in Lusaka and intend to serve customers across Zambia, with scope to expand beyond Zambia as the business grows.

Our purpose

We started with a simple idea: make buying and sourcing products easier. Customers should not have to contact several suppliers just to find an item, compare prices or arrange a purchase.

Who we serve

Zambian households, professionals, SMEs and organisations that want a simpler way to shop or source products online.

Why choose us

  • Designed around the realities of buying and sourcing in Zambia, rather than copying an overseas marketplace model.
  • One marketplace intended to span household, consumer and business purchasing.
  • Access for both individual and organisational buyers.
  • Opportunities for Zambian suppliers and businesses to reach more customers.
