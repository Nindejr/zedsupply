Ordering

  1. Find or request it. Search, shop by category, or send us a request for anything you can't find.
  2. Checkout on WhatsApp. Your cart is sent to us as an itemised order with an order number.
  3. We confirm. We confirm the final price, delivery and payment with you before you pay.
  4. We see it through. We connect your order to the right seller or supplier and support it through to fulfilment. You can follow it under Orders or Track order.

Keeping your payment details safe

  • Zed Supply will never ask for your card number, card security code (CVV), PIN or Mobile Money PIN on this website, by email, by phone or on WhatsApp.
  • If anyone claiming to be Zed Supply asks for them, don't share them. Tell us straight away.
  • Our website blocks card numbers from being sent through its forms, so they can't be sent to us by mistake.
  • When online card payments open, you'll pay on our payment provider's secure page. Zed Supply won't see or store your full card details.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to pay Zed Supply?

Yes. We confirm every order with you on WhatsApp before you pay, and we never ask for card numbers, security codes or PINs. See Keeping your payment details safe.

Are prices final?

Prices are shown in Kwacha. We confirm the final price, including delivery, on WhatsApp before you pay.

Can you find a product that isn't listed?

Yes. Send us a request and we'll look for it from local suppliers.

Can I order from China or other countries?

Not yet. We're putting supplier agreements in place first. See Shop the World.

How do I track my order?

Use Track order with the order number from your WhatsApp message, or ask us on WhatsApp.

What is data saver?

Data saver hides product photos so the site uses less mobile data. Turn it on or off in the All categories menu.

About Zed Supply

Zed Supply is a Zambia-based e-commerce business building an online marketplace for everyday products, business supplies and other goods. We want to make it easier for people and organisations to find what they need, compare options and buy from one place.

We are based in Lusaka and intend to serve customers across Zambia, with scope to expand beyond Zambia as the business grows.

Our purpose

We started with a simple idea: make buying and sourcing products easier. Customers should not have to contact several suppliers just to find an item, compare prices or arrange a purchase.

Who we serve

Zambian households, professionals, SMEs and organisations that want a simpler way to shop or source products online.

Why choose us

  • Designed around the realities of buying and sourcing in Zambia, rather than copying an overseas marketplace model.
  • One marketplace intended to span household, consumer and business purchasing.
  • Access for both individual and organisational buyers.
  • Opportunities for Zambian suppliers and businesses to reach more customers.
