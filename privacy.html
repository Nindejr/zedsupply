2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Quote form: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), the category you choose and the details of your request.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: your name, number or email address and anything you choose to tell us in your messages.
  • Orders: if you go on to buy through us, the details needed to complete the order, such as delivery address and payment confirmation.

Please don't send us sensitive personal data (for example health information or ID numbers) unless we specifically need it for your order.

Information collected automatically

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our web host may record basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in server logs to keep the site secure and working.

3. How and why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation. We do this at your request, before entering into a contract with you.
  • To arrange and complete orders you place with us. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records. This is required by law.
  • To send you updates or offers, only if you have given your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We only collect what we need for these purposes and we do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

  • Suppliers and sellers we connect your order to, only the details they need to quote for or fulfil it.
  • Formspree, which receives quote-form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Google, which provides our email service, the fonts used on this site and the map on our contact page.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy that applies to the data it processes.

5. Transfers outside Zambia

Some of the services above store or process data on servers outside Zambia. We only use these services where appropriate safeguards are in place, and we will transfer your personal data outside Zambia only as permitted by the Act. By using the quote form or contacting us through these services, you agree to this transfer.

6. Cookies and third-party content

Our own website does not set cookies. Two pieces of third-party content may collect technical data when they load:

  • Google Fonts, which loads our typefaces and receives your IP address.
  • Google Maps, embedded on the contact page, which may set its own cookies.

If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy first.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24 months] after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer. Order and financial records are kept for as long as Zambian tax and company law requires. After that, we delete or anonymise the data.

8. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on our email and accounts and limiting access to the people who need it. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal data is used (this policy);
  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data, where we have no lawful reason to keep it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
  • complain to the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

To use any of these rights, email expresszambezi@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within the time the Act allows.

10. Children

This website is not aimed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
