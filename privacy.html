2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), the topic you choose, your message and any delivery area you provide.
  • Seller application: your business name, contact person, phone, email, town, PACRA number (if you give it), the categories you sell in and anything you tell us about your products.
  • Delivery location: the town or city, area and (optionally) street, plot or nearby landmark you enter under "Deliver to". It is added to your WhatsApp orders and enquiries so we can confirm delivery to your area and the delivery fee. We receive it only in messages you choose to send. We never collect GPS location.
  • WhatsApp orders and messages: when you check out or contact us on WhatsApp, we receive your name and number, the products, quantities and prices in your order, and anything you choose to tell us.
  • Orders: if you go on to buy through us, the details needed to complete the order, such as delivery address and payment confirmation.

Information stored only on your device

Your cart, wishlist, recently viewed products, the account details you enter in My Account, order references, reviews you write, Seller Centre demo promotions and deals, the products you add to compare, your data saver setting and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. This preview version of the site does not send them to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by signing out, emptying your cart or clearing your browser's site data.

Your delivery location is kept in your browser only until you close the tab, unless you tick "Remember on this device", in which case it stays on your device until you clear it. The location of each order you send is also kept with that order in your order history on your device. Use "Clear" in the delivery location panel to remove it.

Please don't send us sensitive personal data (for example health information or ID numbers) unless we specifically need it for your order.

Payment card details

We do not collect, store or process payment card details on this website, by email or on WhatsApp, and we will never ask you for them. If a card number is typed into one of our forms, the website stops it from being sent. When online card payments are introduced, they will be handled entirely by a PCI DSS-compliant payment provider on its own secure page, and Zed Supply will not see or store your full card number or security code.

Information collected automatically

If you accept cookies, this website uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, and the advertising tags of Meta, LinkedIn and Google Ads, as described in section 6. If you decline, the advertising tags never load. Our web host may record basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in server logs to keep the site secure and working.

3. How and why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, use any delivery area you provide to assess fulfilment, and prepare a quotation. We do this at your request, before entering into a contract with you.
  • To arrange and complete orders you place with us. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To assess and verify seller applications and set up seller accounts. We do this at your request, before entering into a contract with you.
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records. This is required by law.
  • To send you updates or offers, only if you have given your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • To measure how well our adverts on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Google work, and to show our adverts to people who have visited our website, only if you accept cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time with Cookie settings.

We only collect what we need for these purposes and we do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

  • Suppliers and sellers we connect your order to, only the details they need to quote for or fulfil it.
  • Couriers that deliver your order, chosen by you, the seller or us: only your name, phone number and delivery location.
  • Formspree, which receives contact-form and seller-application submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Google, which provides our email service, the map on our contact page and, if you accept, Google Analytics and Google Ads.
  • Microsoft, which provides Microsoft Clarity (see section 6).
  • LinkedIn (owned by Microsoft), which provides the LinkedIn Insight Tag, if you accept (see section 6).
  • Meta (the company behind Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), which provides WhatsApp, if you choose to message us there, and the Meta Pixel, if you accept (see section 6).
  • Mobile money providers (MTN, Airtel or Zamtel), which process your payment and share with us the details needed to confirm it, such as your name, number, amount and transaction reference.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy that applies to the data it processes.

5. Transfers outside Zambia

Some of the services above store or process data on servers outside Zambia. We only use these services where appropriate safeguards are in place, and we will transfer your personal data outside Zambia only as permitted by the Act. By using our forms or contacting us through these services, you agree to this transfer.

6. Cookies and third-party content

Google Analytics (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use our website, such as which pages are visited and whether people tap our WhatsApp, phone, email or other links, so we can improve it. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics and advertising cookies.

  • If you accept: Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) to recognise repeat visits, and collects information such as the pages you view, the links you tap, your approximate location (from your IP address), and your device and browser type.
  • If you decline, or before you choose: no analytics cookies are set. Google may still receive basic, cookieless signals that a page was viewed or a link was tapped, without anything that identifies your browser between visits.
  • We never send Google your name, phone number, email address, delivery location, WhatsApp messages or what you type in our forms. Links are reported without their message text.
  • Google Analytics itself is not used for advertising. Google Ads, described below, is a separate tool.
  • Your choice is saved in your browser. You can change it at any time with , also found at the bottom of every page.

Microsoft Clarity (cookies only if you accept)

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how people move around our pages, for example where they tap and how far they scroll, so we can find and fix things that are confusing. Clarity creates heatmaps and anonymised replays of visits.

  • If you accept: Clarity sets cookies (named _clck and _clsk) to link the pages of one visit and to recognise repeat visits, and records clicks, taps, scrolling and mouse movement, with your device type, browser and approximate location.
  • If you decline, or before you choose: Clarity runs without cookies and cannot link your visits together.
  • We mask personal details so they never appear in recordings: everything typed into our forms, your name, your delivery location, your cart summary and your orders.
  • Clarity is provided by Microsoft, which may process this data outside Zambia.
  • The same choice controls Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and the advertising tags below.

Advertising tags: Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag and Google Ads (only if you accept)

These tags let us measure the adverts we run on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Google. They tell each platform when a visitor views our pages, sends our contact form, or taps one of our WhatsApp or phone links, so we can see which adverts lead to enquiries. The platforms may also use this to show our adverts to people who have visited our website. Before you choose, or if you decline, none of these tags loads, no advertising cookies are set and nothing is sent to these companies.

  • Meta Pixel, provided by Meta, the company behind Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. It records the pages you view and the three actions above, with your browser, device and IP address. It sets cookies named _fbp and, if you arrived from one of our Facebook or Instagram adverts, _fbc. If you use Facebook or Instagram, Meta may link this to your account. Meta Privacy Policy
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag, provided by LinkedIn, which is owned by Microsoft. It records the pages you view and the three actions above, with your browser, device and IP address, and may link them to your LinkedIn account if you have one. It sets cookies such as li_fat_id on our site and its own cookies on linkedin.com. LinkedIn reports to us only in totals and does not tell us who you are. LinkedIn Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads, provided by Google. It records the pages you view and, if you came from one of our Google adverts, whether you then sent our contact form or tapped one of our WhatsApp or phone links. It sets cookies such as _gcl_au on our site, and Google may use its own cookies on google.com and doubleclick.net. Google Privacy Policy
  • As with Google Analytics, we never send these companies your name, phone number, email address, delivery location, WhatsApp messages or what you type in our forms. They learn only that an action happened, for example that the contact form was sent.
  • Meta, LinkedIn and Google may process this data outside Zambia (see section 5).
  • Choosing Decline in stops these tags straight away and deletes their cookies on our site. Cookies they have set on their own websites can be removed in your browser settings, and each platform lets you manage ad preferences in your account settings.

Other third-party content

Apart from the analytics and advertising cookies you have accepted, our own website does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only for the on-device features described in section 2. One piece of third-party content may collect technical data when it loads:

  • Google Maps, embedded in the contact section of our help centre, which may set its own cookies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24 months] after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer. Order and financial records are kept for as long as Zambian tax and company law requires. After that, we delete or anonymise the data.

8. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on our email and accounts and limiting access to the people who need it. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the Act requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal data is used (this policy);
  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data, where we have no lawful reason to keep it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
  • complain to the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

To use any of these rights, email expresszambezi@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond within the time the Act allows.

10. Children

This website is not aimed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
